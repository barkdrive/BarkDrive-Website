Welcome to BarkDrive Cat Grooming

A well-maintained coat helps keep your cat feeling comfortable and cared for.

While cats groom themselves, a little professional help can make all the difference, especially for long-haired coats prone to knots and matting.

Our gentle grooming is tailored to your cat’s individual coat, needs and comfort level, helping them feel fresh, soft and beautifully cared for.

Stand Alone Services

Nail Trim

Regular nail trimming helps prevent overgrown or broken claws, reduces the risk of claws catching or curling into the paw pads, and supports comfortable movement. It also makes routine paw checks easier, helping spot any problems early.

Hygiene Trim

Trimming of fur around the rear and back legs to keep the area hygienic and comfortable, while preventing the buildup of dirt and debris.

Bath

Gently washes away dirt, grease and debrs that can accumulate in the coat leaving your cat feeling clean and refreshed.

Under carriage trim

Shaving the fur on a long-haired cat's belly, armpits, and groin area helps prevent painful matting and hygiene issues.
